# Use Cases

Q1X is intended for broad multi-stage digital work, including:

- forming and launching a company;
- market, legal, IP, operational and commercial research;
- digital R&D programmes and virtual experiments;
- product and service development;
- software and web application delivery;
- cloud, local and hybrid infrastructure programmes;
- documentation, reporting and evidence production;
- agent-assisted business operations;
- multi-model research and independent review;
- workflows that eventually hand off to physical prototyping or devices.

Use cases do not define hard-coded departments. The orchestrator should derive the work structure required by the requested outcome.
